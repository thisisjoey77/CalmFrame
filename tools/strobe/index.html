CalmFrame strobe test page

This page flashes a rectangle on purpose, to calibrate and demonstrate CalmFrame's detector. Nothing flashes until you press Start, and every burst is limited.

  • Do not run this if you have known or suspected photosensitivity, or if anyone nearby does. Ask the room first.
  • Each burst gets a 2-second steady lead-in, then stops by itself.
  • Esc, or a click anywhere, stops a burst immediately.
  • The page stops itself after 5 seconds no matter what the burst length says.
Defaults: a 0.2 luminance swing between two greys (low contrast), 25% of the window, 10 Hz, a 3-second burst. Full contrast is a separate toggle and is off.
